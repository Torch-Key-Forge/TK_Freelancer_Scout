# Torch & Key Freelancer Scout

**Bounded opportunity intelligence for human-reviewed freelance project intake.**

## Purpose

Freelancer Scout is an internal tool that helps Torch & Key find active freelance projects, retrieve the limited project information needed for qualification, and prepare opportunities for human review.

## How the application is used

Torch & Key earns revenue by completing client projects. The application supports that services business by reducing the time required to find and assess relevant opportunities. The Scout itself is not sold, and marketplace data is not resold or monetized.

## Permissions

The initial application is read-only. It searches and retrieves project information and may prepare non-binding recommendations.

It does **not** place bids, send or read messages, accept awards, manage milestones, modify account data, or perform financial or contractual actions.

## Human control

CJ remains the decision-maker. No bid, message, award, project acceptance, milestone action, or other marketplace mutation may occur without CJ's explicit authorization. A match, score, summary, or recommendation never constitutes approval.

## Privacy and data handling

- Credentials and OAuth tokens remain local and are never committed.
- The public sample does not create a database or analytics stream.
- Retrieved data is limited to what is needed for opportunity review.
- Freelancer data is not sold, redistributed, or used to profile marketplace participants.
- Public repository content excludes private receipts, credentials, local configuration, and internal operating doctrine.

See the complete [privacy and permissions note](PRIVACY.md) and the [source overview](README.md).
